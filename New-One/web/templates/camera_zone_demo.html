How it works

  1. Upload a sample video for the camera.
  2. A drawing frame is taken from the video at the same resolution used for detection. Optionally pick a later timestamp if the first frame is unclear.
  3. Open the zone drawer and draw the main pathway (yellow) and mobile-free zones (magenta) on that video frame.
  4. Run detection and watch the live review: pathway mapping and detection boxes play on the uploaded video so you can confirm whether alerts are right or wrong.
